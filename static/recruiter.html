Google Forms applications
Every job created while Google is connected gets its own published form. Responses sync into Applications as candidates, the submitted answers stay on the application, and a CV uploaded to Drive lands in Resume Screening (add a File upload question in Google Forms once — the API cannot create one). The form must collect an email address (form Settings → Responses → Collect email addresses, or an Email question) — a response without one is skipped, because the candidate account and every later email is keyed by it.
